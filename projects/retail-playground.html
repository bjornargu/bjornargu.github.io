In Development

Autonomous multi-agent retail simulation featuring multi-turn sales dialogue and inventory restock audits.

System Overview

A simulated retail business cycle executed by autonomous LLM agents. The runtime orchestrates morning cash verification, multi-turn AI-to-AI customer negotiation, end-of-day threshold-based procurement audits, and ledger reconciliations.

  • Day Lifecycle: Sequential business day model (`run_business_day`) controlling morning briefing, store operations, and closing audit.
  • Multi-Turn Interaction: Direct interaction between `CustomerAgent` and `SalesAgent` with an autonomous exit condition (`[DONE]`) or bounded turn cap.
  • Autonomous Procurement: Goal-driven `ProcurementAgent` tasked with inventory reordering thresholds (< 10 units up to 20) while protecting a cash reserve cushion.
  • Ledger & State Validation: Verification of treasury variance, final SKU counts, and recent ledger activity via core state tools.
